Areas we serve

Spectrum Arch is based in Clifton Park. OPWDD authorizes Support Brokers statewide, and we plan to meet families in person across our primary area: Saratoga, Albany, Schenectady, Rensselaer, Warren, Washington, Fulton, Montgomery, Schoharie, Columbia and Greene counties.

Outside that area? Pick your county for local first steps, and contact us. OPWDD requires in-person meetings unless a virtual meeting is pre-approved in the Life Plan, so we'll talk honestly about whether we can serve you well.
