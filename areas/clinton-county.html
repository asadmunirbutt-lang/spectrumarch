Areas we serve / Region 2

Support Brokerage and Self-Direction in Clinton County, New York

Spectrum Arch is a nonprofit in Clifton Park, in New York's Capital Region. Here is how families in Clinton County can get started with OPWDD Self-Direction, and how to reach us.

Contact usJoin the waitlist

Clinton County at a glance

  • County seat: Plattsburgh
  • OPWDD region: Region 2: Central New York, Broome and North Country
  • OPWDD regional office: Sunmount (North Country)
  • Spectrum Arch service: Contact us to discuss

Can Spectrum Arch serve families in Clinton County?

Possibly. OPWDD authorizes Support Brokers statewide, so we are not limited to the Capital Region. But OPWDD requires the broker and the family to meet in person unless a virtual meeting has been pre-approved and documented in the person's Life Plan, and some of the work goes better face to face.

Clinton County is outside our primary service area around Clifton Park. If you're looking for a Support Broker here, contact us. We'll tell you honestly whether we can serve your family well, including how in-person meetings would work, and if we can't, we'll help you think about how to find a broker closer to home.

Getting started with Self-Direction in Clinton County

  1. OPWDD eligibility. Find the Front Door contact for your area on OPWDD's contact page. Our guide to eligibility and qualifying diagnoses explains what OPWDD reviews.
  2. Medicaid. Apply through the Clinton County Department of Social Services, or through the NY State of Health marketplace. See Medicaid and OPWDD services.
  3. A Care Manager. Choose a Care Coordination Organization that serves Clinton County. See questions to ask a CCO.
  4. Request Self-Direction, then choose a Support Broker and a Fiscal Intermediary. Our step-by-step guide walks through it.
  5. Children under 3? Start with Clinton County's Early Intervention program. See Early Intervention in New York.

Also served by the Sunmount (North Country) office

Essex County, Franklin County, Hamilton County, Jefferson County, St. Lawrence County. See all 62 counties.
