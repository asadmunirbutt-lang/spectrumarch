An easy way to remember it

The Care Manager plans your overall services. The Support Broker designs your self-direction budget and helps you run it. The FI makes the money and paperwork work.

Where families get stuck

  • "Who do I call about a late paycheck?" The FI.
  • "Can we add a class to the budget?" Your Support Broker.
  • "Our Medicaid letter says coverage is ending." Your Care Manager, right away.
  • "We want a different Support Broker." Your Care Manager can help you switch.
