For Care Managers: Referring a Family for Support Brokerage

Care Managers are often the first to know a family needs a Support Broker. Here's how to refer a family to us, and how we'll work with you.

Last reviewed October 8, 2026

Where we are

Spectrum Arch is a Clifton Park nonprofit in the process of offering Support Brokerage, and we are completing OPWDD's Support Broker training and authorization. We're not serving families yet, but we're building a waitlist so families have a broker lined up when we can begin.

Who we expect to serve

  • People eligible for OPWDD services and enrolled in the HCBS Waiver, or close to it
  • Families who want to self-direct with Budget Authority, including those already self-directing and looking for a new broker
  • Families in Saratoga, Albany, Schenectady, Rensselaer, and the surrounding counties
  • Help in English, Urdu, Hindi, Punjabi, and Pashto

How to refer a family

  1. Get the family's permission to share their contact details with us.
  2. Use our waitlist form: choose "A Care Manager or other professional", and put the family's preferred name and contact details in the note. Or email info@spectrumarch.org.
  3. Keep it minimal. Please don't send diagnoses, Medicaid numbers, or records by email. We'll arrange a secure way to exchange anything needed later.

How we'll work with you

  • We'll confirm we received the referral.
  • When we're able to begin, we'll contact the family directly and keep you informed with their consent.
  • We'll coordinate with you on the Life Plan and work alongside the family's chosen Fiscal Intermediary.

Questions? Call 317-991-0361 or email info@spectrumarch.org.

Looking for a Support Broker in the Capital Region?

Spectrum Arch is in the process of offering Support Brokerage across Saratoga, Albany, Schenectady, Rensselaer and the surrounding counties. Join the waitlist and we will contact you as soon as we can begin.
