OPWDD Self-Direction help for New York families

Self-Direction lets a person with a developmental disability choose their own supports, staff, and schedule. Here is how it works in plain language, and how Spectrum Arch can help, at no cost to talk.

The path, step by step

  1. Confirm OPWDD eligibility

    Apply through OPWDD's Front Door. This usually needs records showing a developmental disability that began before age 22.

  2. Enroll in Medicaid and the HCBS Waiver

    Self-Direction is available to people enrolled in Medicaid and OPWDD's Home and Community-Based Services Waiver.

  3. Choose a Care Manager

    Your Care Manager, from a Care Coordination Organization, writes your Life Plan and helps you request Self-Direction.

  4. Choose a Support Broker and a Fiscal Intermediary

    Your broker helps you plan and build the budget. Your Fiscal Intermediary handles payroll and pays approved expenses.

  5. Plan and build your budget

    With your Circle of Support, set goals and turn them into a self-direction budget to submit for approval.

  6. Hire staff and start services

    Once the budget is approved, you hire staff through your FI, and your broker helps you adjust as needs change.

Timing varies, and each step takes time, so it helps to start early, ideally well before school services end at 21.
