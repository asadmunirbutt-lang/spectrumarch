Areas we serve

Spectrum Arch is based in Clifton Park, in Saratoga County. We plan to meet families in person across eleven Capital Region counties, and we're glad to talk with any New York family about Self-Direction.

Our in-person service area

These are the counties we can reach comfortably from Clifton Park for home visits, planning meetings, and the in-person meetings OPWDD expects. All of them are in OPWDD's Region 3.

Can a Support Broker work outside their county?

Yes. OPWDD authorizes Support Brokers to work anywhere in New York State, not county by county. When brokers apply, they choose the regions and counties they're willing to serve, and that authorization is renewed every year.

What limits distance is the meetings. OPWDD requires the Support Broker and the person to meet in person, unless a virtual meeting has been approved ahead of time and written into the person's Life Plan. A good broker also visits, gets to know the household, and knows local staff, programs, and respite options. That's hard to do from three hours away, which is why we focus on the Capital Region.

Every part of the state is covered by one of OPWDD's five regions, each with its own regional office and Front Door, the starting point for eligibility and services:

To reach the Front Door in your area, use OPWDD's contact page. In the Capital District, the Front Door number is 518-388-0398.

You're still welcome to contact us. We're happy to talk with families anywhere in New York and will tell you honestly whether we can serve you, or help you think about finding a Support Broker closer to home. Our guide to choosing a Support Broker lists the questions to ask.
