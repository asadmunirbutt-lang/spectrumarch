Support Brokerage across the Capital Region

Spectrum Arch is based in Clifton Park, Saratoga County. We are building Support Brokerage services for families throughout the Capital Region of New York.

Counties and communities

Saratoga County

Including Saratoga Springs, Clifton Park, Halfmoon, Malta, Ballston Spa, Wilton, Mechanicville, and Waterford.

Albany County

Including Albany, Colonie, Latham, Guilderland, Bethlehem, Delmar, Cohoes, and Watervliet.

Schenectady County

Including Schenectady, Niskayuna, Glenville, Scotia, and Rotterdam.

Rensselaer County

Including Troy, Rensselaer, East Greenbush, North Greenbush, and Brunswick.

Warren County

Including Glens Falls, Queensbury, and Lake George.

Washington County

Including Hudson Falls, Fort Edward, Greenwich, and Granville.

Columbia County

Including Hudson, Chatham, Kinderhook, and Valatie.

Greene County

Including Catskill, Coxsackie, and Cairo.

Don't see your town? Contact us. If we can't help, we will point you to someone who can.

Local help matters

A Support Broker who knows the area can connect you with nearby community activities, day programs, respite options, and staff, and can meet in person when that helps. Spectrum Arch is based in Clifton Park and focused on the Capital Region.

Support Brokerage at Spectrum Arch is in process; see our Support Brokerage page for where things stand.
