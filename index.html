Building the Arch to Independence

Support Brokerage for families who self-direct in the Capital Region

OPWDD Self-Direction lets people with developmental disabilities choose their own supports, staff, and schedule. A Support Broker helps you plan it, budget it, and keep it running. Spectrum Arch is a local nonprofit getting ready to do exactly that.

Join the waitlist How Support Brokerage works

Self-Direction gives families real choices. It also comes with a lot of paperwork.

With Self-Direction you can hire people you trust, build a week around the person instead of a program's schedule, and spend an approved budget on what actually helps. To get there, someone has to build a Circle of Support, turn goals into a budget, get it approved, hire staff through a Fiscal Intermediary, and keep it all on track year after year.

That is the Support Broker's job. People who self-direct with Budget Authority work with one.

A Support Broker helps you:

  • Understand your Self-Direction options in plain language
  • Bring together your Circle of Support
  • Turn Life Plan goals into a self-direction budget
  • Find, hire, and keep good staff
  • Adjust the plan as needs change, and stay ready for annual renewals

Where we are

Spectrum Arch is in the process of offering Support Brokerage in Saratoga County and across the Capital Region, and we are working through OPWDD's Support Broker training and authorization. We are not serving Self-Direction clients yet. Join the waitlist and we will contact you as soon as we can begin.

Why join the waitlist now?

Getting Self-Direction started takes time: eligibility, waiver enrollment, a Care Manager, and a budget. If you join now, we can talk through where you are in that process and contact you as soon as we are authorized to begin.

Joining is free and doesn't commit you to anything.
