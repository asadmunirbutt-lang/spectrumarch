What a Support Broker does

If you self-direct your OPWDD services with Budget Authority, you work with a Support Broker. Your broker:

  • Explains your Self-Direction options in plain language
  • Brings together your Circle of Support: the people who know you best
  • Turns the goals in your Life Plan into a budget and gets it submitted for approval
  • Helps you find and hire staff through your Fiscal Intermediary
  • Adjusts the plan as needs change and keeps you ready for annual renewals

How it works with Spectrum Arch

  1. Join our list

    It takes about two minutes. We don't ask for diagnoses or medical details.

  2. We talk it through

    A free call or email about where you are: eligibility, waiver enrollment, your Care Manager, and what you need.

  3. We match you with a broker

    We introduce you to an OPWDD-authorized broker who works with Spectrum Arch, based on your county, needs, and language.

  4. Your broker gets started

    Your broker helps build your Circle of Support, plan, and first budget. Brokerage during this start-up phase is funded separately and doesn't come out of your budget.

Is this right for your family?

  • The person has been found eligible for OPWDD services, or you've applied
  • You're enrolled in the HCBS Waiver, or working on it
  • You want to self-direct, or you already do and want a new broker
  • You live in Saratoga, Albany, Schenectady, Rensselaer, Warren, Washington, Fulton, Montgomery, Schoharie, Columbia, or Greene County

Not sure where you stand? That's what the free conversation is for. Families elsewhere in New York are welcome to ask too.

Questions families ask

Do families pay for Support Brokerage?

Usually not out of pocket. Support Brokerage is an OPWDD waiver service funded through Medicaid. Start-up brokerage is funded separately; after that, brokerage is paid from your Self-Direction budget. See the full FAQ.

Do we need OPWDD eligibility first?

Self-Direction starts after the person is found eligible for OPWDD services and enrolled in the HCBS Waiver. If you're still applying, join anyway and we'll talk through the steps. Our eligibility guide explains what OPWDD looks at.

Can we change brokers later?

Yes. You choose your Support Broker, and you can change if the fit isn't right.
