Medicaid Rides to Medical Appointments in Upstate New York: How to Book

By Asad M. Butt, President, Spectrum Arch, Inc. · November 9, 2026 · 1 min read

TransportationMedicaidHealth care

Missing a doctor's, dentist's, or therapy appointment because there's no ride is more common than it should be. If your family member has Medicaid in New York, transportation to Medicaid-covered medical appointments may be covered too.

What's covered

New York Medicaid covers non-emergency medical transportation to Medicaid-covered services when a person needs it to get there. The type of ride depends on need, from public transit to a taxi or car service to a wheelchair van.

Emergencies are different: in an emergency, call 911.

How booking works upstate

In upstate New York, including the Capital Region, Medicaid trips are arranged through the state's transportation manager, Medical Answering Services (MAS). You can book by phone or online at medanswering.com, which lists the phone number for your county.

  • Book ahead. Plan on calling at least three days before the appointment.
  • Have ready: the person's Medicaid ID, the appointment date and time, the provider's name and address, and any mobility needs.
  • Standing orders can be set up for repeating appointments, such as weekly therapy.

If your family member is in a Medicaid managed care plan, check the plan's member handbook as well. It explains how transportation works for that plan.

Tips for riders with disabilities

  • Mention if the person needs an attendant, extra time, or help to the door.
  • Prepare the person for the ride: who will pick them up, what the vehicle looks like, and how long it will take.
  • Keep a card with the person's name, address, appointment, and an emergency contact.
  • Allow for pickup windows, and tell the doctor's office if a ride is running late.

For everyday trips that aren't medical, such as to work or activities, see CDTA STAR paratransit.

Sources: NYS DOH, Medicaid Transportation Program Overview; NYS DOH, Medicaid Enrollee FAQs; Medical Answering Services.
