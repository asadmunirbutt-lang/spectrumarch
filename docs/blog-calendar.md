# Blog plan: 50 topics

Not published; this folder is never deployed. Target 2–3 posts a week, not one a day. A steady pace of useful, reviewed posts is what Google and the Ad Grants team reward; a burst of daily posts from a new site looks like mass-produced content.

**Every post before it goes live:**
1. Facts checked against a primary source (OPWDD, NYSED, SSA, NY law) and linked at the bottom.
2. Read and edited by Asad (or the named author). Add at least one real detail from your own experience in your own words: something you've seen, a question families ask you, a local place or program.
3. No invented stories, quotes, families, or statistics.
4. Written for a parent reading on a phone at night: plain words, short sections, a clear next step.

**Needs you** = a post that only works with your first-hand input (story, photos, local knowledge). Send notes or a voice memo and it gets drafted from those.
