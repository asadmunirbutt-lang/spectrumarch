## Which platforms, in order

1. **Facebook Page.** Where Capital Region parents and caregiver groups already are. Highest priority.
2. **LinkedIn Page.** For partners, funders, OPWDD providers, and hiring Direct Support Professionals.
3. **Instagram.** Real photos of events and the community. Works best once you have photos to share.
4. **Google Business Profile** (business.google.com). Not social media, but it puts you on Google Maps and in local search.
5. **X and YouTube.** Optional. Claim the name so nobody else takes it, but don't spread yourself thin.

## Keeping it going

- **Share each new blog post on the day it goes live.** The schedule is in `docs/blog-calendar.md`.
- Aim for 2–3 posts a week. Consistency matters more than volume.
- Post real things when they happen: certification milestones, community events, board meetings, volunteer days.
- Reply to comments and messages within a day or two.
- Never post photos of anyone, especially residents or children, without written consent.
- Don't buy followers or likes. It hurts reach and credibility, including with Google.

## When the pages are live

Send the links. They'll be added to the website footer and to the site's structured data, which tells Google these accounts belong to Spectrum Arch.
